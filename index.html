Our Culinary Journey

A Kolhapur Favorite Built on Fire, Flavor & Generosity

Founded in 2019 right near the scenic Rankala Lake in Sambhaji Nagar, The Chinese Factory started with a simple mission: to serve bold, hygienic, and piping-hot Indo-Chinese food that matches Kolhapur’s love for rich spices and generous portions.

Whether you are stopping by after an evening stroll at Rankala Lake or ordering a family feast at home, every dish is cooked to order in high-heat seasoned woks using farm-fresh vegetables, tender meats, and our signature house-blended sauces.
